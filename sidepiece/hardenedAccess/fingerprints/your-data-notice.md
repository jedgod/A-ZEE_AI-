# Your data

We collect a small amount of technical information to prevent abuse of the free plan:

- **Device cookie** — a random identifier stored in an HttpOnly cookie (`az_dev`) so we can recognize the same browser.
- **Browser fingerprint** — a hash of basic, non-invasive properties (user agent, language, screen size, timezone). This is not used to identify you across the internet.
- **IP address** — recorded only when the request comes through a trusted proxy. An IP address by itself is never used to link accounts.

These signals are used solely to detect when multiple free accounts appear to come from the same device or browser. If we detect that pattern after free allowance has been used, we may ask you to upgrade. Paying customers are never affected.

Signals are kept for 90 days and then deleted. You can request deletion of your account data at any time from your account settings.

We do not sell this information or use it for advertising.
