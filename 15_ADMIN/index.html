15_ADMIN / GOVERNANCE

Operate the platform with control.

This governance layer adds tenant management, approval queues, role-based access control, registry review, policy oversight, billing visibility, and incident response. It turns the system from an execution engine into a managed, audit-ready operating environment.

Tenants

Allocate workspaces, enforce boundaries, and assign ownership at the org level.

Users & Roles

Manage owner, admin, reviewer, operator, and viewer privileges with explicit scope.

Approvals

Review model changes, extension enablement, policy releases, and workflow access requests.

Policies

Apply and track enforcement rules for compliance, input safety, and permissions.

Registry

Maintain approved model versions, restricted releases, and governance state.

Billing

Monitor spend, token usage, storage, and latency budgets across the platform.

Incidents

Escalate operational issues, track status, and maintain action visibility for the team.

Dashboard

Provide a single control surface for metrics, approval load, and operational health.
